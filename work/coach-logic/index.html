What we built.

Operations dashboards

One dashboard per person, built around their job rather than a template. Each opens on what that person needs to act on first.

A CRM built for how they sell

Built rather than bought, and shaped around how the team actually sells to its clients.

  • A clear pipeline, with every deal owned and moving
  • A view after the sale, so client accounts are looked after, not forgotten
  • Linked accounts, so related clients sit together

Meetings that turn into tasks

Meetings are captured by an AI note taker, set up so every summary comes out in the same structure: decisions, actions, owners, deal context. Custom agents then file the notes against the right deal and turn agreed actions into owned tasks on each person's dashboard.

Every deal links both ways to its conversations and its next steps, so opening a deal shows its whole history.

Research that ranks who to call

Desk research turned into a ranked target list, scored from public data. The scoring rules are visible, so they can be changed and re-run.

The content engine

Marketing campaigns are designed and built from start to finish, then written and scheduled automatically. Every post arrives ready to go. All the team has to do is approve it.

A client portal

A secure space where each client gets their own page: their tasks, resources and progress in one place.

Each client sees only their own information, with checks in place so nothing reaches the wrong client.

Project tracking

One board for every project across the company, with board and timeline views.

The AI layer

AI agents handle recurring admin like meeting follow-up, content drafts and research. Each one checks before writing and asks before acting.

What it means for the team.
